## Owned source and contract

New cpu_tlb_aperture.[ch], cpu_tlb.[ch], cpu_tlb_stress.[ch]; only cpu_bringup.c hooks in existing production files. No mem.c, generic VM, Core scheduler/header/assembly, main, NT consumer or Supervisor edits.

A fixed 4KiB aperture at ffff802000000000 requires an absent leaf under the existing shared direct-map PML4 entry, live PMM table/frame allocations and full retained firmware RAM coverage before INIT. PS ancestors are rejected. Two payload frames and any new lower tables remain owned for the entire domain lifetime, including failure. Published aperture fields are compared with the retained original descriptor before any table dereference.

A sole BSP writer atomically replaces the leaf, performs local INVLPG, and publishes one exact generation. The F1 consumer validates the immutable VA/root/mask and real physical identity, performs INVLPG, then stores completion. Legacy verify/delivered counters and withheld-verify semantics remain separate. Exact generation/state are one atomic 64-bit CAS word; late G cannot poison or complete G+1. Issued generation/request are atomic, mutable payload index is BSP-only, recipient cannot poison, duplicate ACK returns before descriptor reads. Generation overflow is rejected at 2^62-1 before mutation. No tickets, allocations, callbacks or waits in recipient service. No ticket held while dispatching or waiting.

Poison applies only to exact PENDING(G): current pending send/completion failure or deadline handling poisons G and blocks another begin. Successful finish verifies every target's exact INVLPG-before-ACK and transitions G to IDLE. A subsequent BSP QA read/collect failure retains all payload frames/tables and cancels the sole stress writer; its poison attempt does not change completed G from IDLE. A new-frame mismatch at the pre-retirement read checkpoint prevents that round's payload retirement. A mismatch sampled after retirement is recorded in persistent bad metadata without immediate cancellation; it inhibits the next round's retirement, or rejects final component evidence on the last round. There is no generic post-completion abort latch, later-begin rejection guarantee for completed QA errors, teardown quiescence or old-root reclamation proof.

Explicit shz.tlb=test QA warms every physical participant, replaces real PTEs, verifies new payload, then poisons retired payload only after completion and reads. No-invalidate and withheld-completion controls refuse completion and retain all resources. The original no-invalidate evaluator additionally demanded stale hardware payload; that original failed result is preserved. This is an architecture/MM primitive milestone, not full MM or SMP acceptance.
