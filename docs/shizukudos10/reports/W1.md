## Observed (not yet explained)

- **One hang of the clang toolchain probe** (`probe_clang`, 4 `std::thread`s). First run, with the ICU build and the
  gates running on the same 4-core host: the process never finished; the 300 s autorun timeout report shows the main
  thread waiting on the first thread's handle and that thread (`tid 76`, state 1 = runnable) at `probe+1cc9`, the
  `movl %eax, 0x8(%r14)` that stores into its `thread_local` block (`r14` = `ThreadLocalStoragePointer[_tls_index]`);
  `faulted=1` in the final autorun line. Reruns: 6 of 6 PASS (5 of them with the WebKit build loading all 4 cores). A
  lost wake-up or a repeating fault on the new thread's TLS page are both consistent with the report; not enough to
