| build time | 2 m 22 s (`time python3 shizukudos/win64/webkit/deps/toolchain.py --clean`, 4 cores) |

Why not the MSVC ABI (clang `-target x86_64-pc-windows-msvc` + vcruntime140, as `win64/build.py` does for its C++
tests)? WebKit needs a complete C++20 standard library. The MSVC STL headers and the UCRT/vcruntime headers are
Microsoft's and are not available here; libc++ in MSVC mode also needs the vcruntime headers. mingw-w64 + libc++ is a
complete, freely licensed C/C++ runtime that targets the same UCRT DLL. The decision belongs to W1; section 7 lists what
changes if W1 chooses otherwise.
