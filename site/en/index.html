Where ShizukuOS is heading

Your OS.
A new possibility.

Beyond a boot screen, toward an OS for everyday use. These are development goals; complete functional verification is still pending.

Explore the architecture and goals ↗
01

Make it feel like you.

Slade brings a glass-inspired sense of depth as the default theme goal, alongside the refined Flute, experimental Jade and custom themes. Sound and cursors are part of the same experience.

02

Everyday work, naturally.

The native app goals connect the web in Terrasphere, music and video in Muzik, images in Sapphire and documents in Folio.

03

One OS. More possibilities.

ShizukuOS Core connects drivers, the native runtime, Linux Subsystem and ShizukuVM. Windows 10 x86/x64 API compatibility is also a development goal.
